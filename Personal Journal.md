# 1

  

Living organisms cover the soil

Passing moments of uncontrollable recoil

Bathing in rot and fairies 

Packed into a long mortal coil

It's a pandemic, not an isolated incident

Valor of the normotopic is tested

Paladins standing in bemusement

Silence at the rooftops, chaos below

The verdict is set, the contract is void

Verdandi beckons the mortal world

  
  

# 2

towered over fame

Tossing down one more line,

whisking catatonic ponds

When the last, finds the past,

There'll be no tomorrow

Equity over sanctity is the surrender of the soul

Vanishing mists of a tragedy,

levitating on violent energy

Chants of the monsters, dance in clusters 

Favorite divine imposters,

banished and mourned

# 3

  

Cradle of a panoramic view

The narrative falls short of the new

Pyromantic broadcasts and solemn swears 

Bridging the gap of the unknown vessel

Post-induction cataclysmic steward

The burning ship is fuel for the new

Validation not fornication

Parables of a long burning flare

  

# 4

  

Incredibly close to time zero 

Rise of seven dragons and thirty birds

Volcanic ashes in the air

Second and third birth

Pale shadows around a vortex 

Fire flows where the wind goes

Sanctimonious serenity amidst arrested implosion 

Visual condition of a walking corpse

Bisotun is raised, boulevard of ancient dust

Purified in lighting and speaking in tongues 

Helions in a standstill, bountiful lust and greed

The roads remain while caravans bleed 

  

# 5

  

Justice born of non-linear prints

A cawl sent to a worm

Nearest borrowing point

Patient for a window 

Delayed but never late

Panic lobotomization 

is funny from the outside

Pointing to gorgeous viscerality

Genomic bonds of neverending games

The bonds of a leveling face

Books are written, stories born

On the stage of singing storms

Valleys and names, periscopes of fate

Surely it'll remain, before November's rain
