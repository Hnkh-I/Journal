
*Use these to maintain high energy without burning out during work blocks.*

| Technique | The Mechanism | Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Mental Clutch** (Panoramic Gaze) | **Dilate Vision:** Switch from sharp foveal focus (Code) to soft peripheral vision (Docs). Keeps the "engine" revving but disengages the gears. | **Switching Context:** When looking up docs, waiting for installs, or checking a notification. |
| **Pulse & Glide** (Micro-Cycles) | **High/Low Tension:** 5-10m of "Pulse" (High focus/Shallow breath) followed by 2-3m of "Glide" (Low focus/Long exhale). | **Endurance:** Preventing "Tunneling" during long coding or trading sessions. |
| **The Breath Metronome** (Carrier Wave) | **Rhythmic Continuity:** Maintain a steady 5s-In / 5s-Out breath pattern regardless of what the screen is doing. | **Reducing Friction:** When interrupted or changing windows. Bridges the gap so the brain doesn't "Stop/Start." |
| **Liquid Posture** (The Flow Cue) | **Physical Shift:** Lean forward for "Attack" (Output), Lean back for "Intake" (Input). | **Overcoming Blocks:** When you hit a bug or wall. Physically signals "Flow around" rather than "Crash." |
